## 4. `RecTExists` and the boundary of the local correspondence

The local field in the statement is \(k=E_w\), a finite-place completion of a number field, with a valuation subring of the chosen algebraic closure lying above \(w\). `IrrGL k` is a dependent sum over ranks of smooth irreducible representation classes. `WD h` is a quotient by isomorphism of finite-dimensional complex Weil–Deligne representations.
